# Amendment dated 1 July 2026

Effective date: 1 July 2026.

1. This amendment supersedes the meal cap in `meals.md` for expenses dated on or after 1 July 2026.

2. From 1 July 2026, meals incurred for work are **covered** up to $40 per person per day.

3. All other meal rules in `meals.md` still apply. The cap remains per person per day. Alcohol stays **not_covered**. Service charge stays **covered**. Tips stay **not_covered**.

4. Expenses dated before 1 July 2026 stay under the $35 cap in `meals.md`.
