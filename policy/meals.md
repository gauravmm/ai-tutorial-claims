# Meals

1. Meals incurred for work are **covered** up to $35 per person per day.

2. The cap in clause 1 is per person per day, not per receipt.

3. If meal receipts on one day add up to more than the cap, the claim is **partial**. Claim only up to the cap.

4. Alcohol is never claimable. A meal that includes alcohol is **partial**. Claim the food and any service charge. Do not claim the alcohol.

5. Service charge on a meal is **covered**, subject to the daily cap.

6. Tips on a meal are **not_covered**.
