# Harbour Digital Pte Ltd staff expense policy

## General rules

1. This policy covers staff expense claims for Harbour Digital Pte Ltd.

2. The claim period is 1 March 2026 through 31 July 2026 inclusive. A receipt dated outside this period is **not_covered**.

3. Staff must attach a receipt for any expense above $20. An expense above $20 with no receipt is **not_covered**.

4. All amounts in this policy are in SGD. Claims in any other currency are **not_covered**.

5. GST is reclaimable only from a valid tax invoice. A valid tax invoice shows a GST registration number. If the receipt has no GST registration number, the gross amount may still be **covered** or **partial**. The GST reclaim itself is **not_covered**.

6. Do not claim the same expense twice. A second claim for the same expense is **not_covered**.

7. Use one of these verdict words for every line you review:
   - **covered**: the expense is claimable in full under this policy
   - **partial**: only part of the expense is claimable
   - **not_covered**: the expense is not claimable
   - **needs_approval**: the expense is claimable only after the named approval

8. Category rules live in the other files in this folder. A dated amendment supersedes the file it names, for expenses on or after its effective date.
